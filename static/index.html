❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how Sarvam STT, FAISS, and Gemini Flash power VoiceRAG

How does multilingual voice search work?
When you record audio in Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, or English, it is streamed to Sarvam AI's Saaras v3 STT model. The transcript is converted to a vector embedding using MiniLM-L12, matched against indexed passages via FAISS, and passed to Gemini Flash to generate a grounded answer in the same language.
How are hallucinations prevented?
VoiceRAG implements a 5-Pillar Enterprise AI Defense suite: strict prompt lockdown, prompt leak protection, zero-tolerance illegal content filtering, code injection isolation, and token-overlap hallucination checking. If context confidence is below 0.35, the system formally refuses to answer.
What is the latency SLA promise?
Our P50 latency target for the complete retrieval and generation pipeline (excluding audio transmission) is under 800ms. You can track live latency metrics in the Analytics dashboard above.
Is my voice recording stored permanently?
No. Audio data is processed in-memory solely for transcription and is immediately discarded after generating the transcript. We prioritize user privacy and zero permanent audio retention.

Privacy Policy & Responsible AI Charter

Operational Boundaries, Zero-Retention Voice Privacy & 5-Pillar Guardrails

1. ZERO DATA RETENTION

🎙️ Ephemeral Voice Processing & Biometric Privacy

VartaLaap (वार्तालाप) is engineered with a strict Zero-Data-Retention policy for all voice inputs:

  • In-Memory Processing: Audio streams (WAV, WebM, Opus) recorded through your browser mic are processed ephemerally in RAM and forwarded directly to the Sarvam AI speech model.
  • No Audio Storage: Audio recordings are never saved to disk, persistent databases, or telemetry logs.
  • Zero Biometric Harvesting: No voiceprints, pitch analysis, speaker identification, or acoustic biomarkers are ever collected or stored.
2. GOVERNANCE SUITE

🏰 5-Pillar Enterprise Safety & Operational Restrictions

To ensure responsible AI interactions, our pipeline enforces 5 real-time defense pillars on every query:

Pillar 1: Anti-Jailbreak

Blocks adversarial prompt injections, DAN exploits, roleplay bypasses, and unauthorized persona overrides.

Pillar 2: Secret Protection

Protects backend system prompts, environment secrets, and internal endpoints against extraction attacks.

Pillar 3: Actionable Safety

Zero-tolerance rejection of weapon fabrication, malware creation, cyberattack tutorials, and illicit substances.

Pillar 4: Structural Isolation

Neutralizes template injection (Jinja2, template literals) and treats all user input strictly as inert natural language strings.

Pillar 5: Output Verification & PII Redaction

Pre-transmission scanner actively checks and redacts leaked API tokens, PAN/Aadhaar patterns, internal filepaths, or database connection strings.

3. TRANSPARENCY & CITATIONS

📚 Hybrid Grounding & General Knowledge Fallback

  • In-Domain Grounding: Questions regarding indexed topics (Indian languages, geography, governance, Hacker House Goa) are answered strictly from retrieved vector chunks and cited with exact passage scores.
  • General Knowledge Reasoning: If a query falls outside the local dataset, the system transparently notes [Source: General AI Knowledge] and suppresses unrelated vector noise.
  • Hallucination Defense: Responses undergo token-overlap validation before rendering to flag low-grounded claims.
4. ACCEPTABLE USE

⚖️ User Rights & Permitted Interactions

Users are free to test queries in 5 Indic languages, perform latency benchmarks, and ask general knowledge questions. Exploitative probing, denial-of-service attempts, or distribution of harmful payloads will result in immediate IP rate-limiting and connection termination.
